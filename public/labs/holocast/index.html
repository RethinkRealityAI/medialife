Holocast

Put a holo projector in your room. Messages, drawings, photos and polls from any phone or laptop beam into its floating screens, live.

The hologram runs on your phone. Scan to open it there. You can run the control room right here.

Your room is live

      
    
        
  1. Place it. Point at the floor or a table. Pinch to size the projector, then tap Place. Pinch again any time to make it bigger or smaller.
    2. 
        
  2. Watch it fill. Anything sent from the control room beams up into the screens: notices, live drawing, photos, polls and spoken captions.
    3. 
        
  3. Tap things. Tap a screen to pull it closer, spin the globe and tap a pin to play Bobo's clip, tap a poll bar to vote, and send reactions from the right.
    4. 
        
  4. Invite (bottom left) shows a QR a friend can scan from your screen, to watch with you or to beam things in from their own phone.
