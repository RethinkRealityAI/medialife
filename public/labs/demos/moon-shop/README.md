# Moon Shop (moon-shop)

Source: the legacy 8th Wall sample project `shop` (A-Frame or three.js), rebuilt in plain three.js on the shared
Labs shell (`../shared/`). What was kept and what changed is written at the top of `main.js`.

Assets (in `../media/`): moon/ (moon-ground, platform, portal, skybox), plus car.glb, stereo-glasses.glb, sand-castle.glb from the other demos.

Credits: From the shop sample (models reworked by the sample authors): Rocks by carlosbarrera_10 (Sketchfab), Platform by REX-Hsu (CGTrader, free). The Flag (ManySince910) is not used (20 MB).

Engine: 8th Wall engine binary 1.0.0 (world tracking), three.js r180. Licences are on the Licences page (menu > Licences, `/evade/licences/`), never on screen.

Status: simulator-tested only (headless Chromium, fake camera, faked motion). Not tested on a phone yet.
