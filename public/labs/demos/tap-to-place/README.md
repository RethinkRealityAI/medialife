# Street Kit (tap-to-place)

Source: the legacy 8th Wall sample project `taptoplace` (A-Frame or three.js), rebuilt in plain three.js on the shared
Labs shell (`../shared/`). What was kept and what changed is written at the top of `main.js`.

Assets (in `../media/`): street/ (bench, lantern, trashcan glTF + bins + shared texture, tray icons drawn by us).

Credits: Low-poly street models from the taptoplace sample (Tiny Treats style pack; original author not stated in the sample).

Engine: 8th Wall engine binary 1.0.0 (world tracking), three.js r180. Licences are on the Licences page (menu > Licences, `/evade/licences/`), never on screen.

Status: simulator-tested only (headless Chromium, fake camera, faked motion). Not tested on a phone yet.
