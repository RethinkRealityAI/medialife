# Pin Bowl (physics)

Source: the legacy 8th Wall sample project `physics-collider` (A-Frame or three.js), rebuilt in plain three.js on the shared
Labs shell (`../shared/`). What was kept and what changed is written at the top of `main.js`.

Assets (in `../media/`): pin.glb, soccer.jpg.

Credits: Pins and ball texture from the physics-collider sample. Physics: cannon-es 0.20.0 (MIT) from jsDelivr. The sample's boxing glove (Pedro Perim) is not used.

Engine: 8th Wall engine binary 1.0.0 (world tracking), three.js r180, cannon-es 0.20.0. Licences are on the Licences page (menu > Licences, `/evade/licences/`), never on screen.

Status: simulator-tested only (headless Chromium, fake camera, faked motion). Not tested on a phone yet.
