# Face FX (face-effects)

Source: the legacy 8th Wall sample project `face-effects` (A-Frame or three.js), rebuilt in plain three.js on the shared
Labs shell (`../shared/`). What was kept and what changed is written at the top of `main.js`.

Assets (in `../media/`): stereo-glasses.glb, head-occluder.glb, face-alpha.png.

Credits: Stereo glasses by jau0gan (TurboSquid, free), as credited by the sample. Face paint and mask art are our own (the sample's tattoo was the engine maker's logo).

Engine: open-source 8th Wall engine (MIT) face chunk, vendored at /evade/keychain-ar/vendor/xr/, three.js r180. Licences are on the Licences page (menu > Licences, `/evade/licences/`), never on screen.

Status: simulator-tested only (headless Chromium, fake camera, faked motion). Not tested on a phone yet.
