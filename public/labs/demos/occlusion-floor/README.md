# Floor Lift (occlusion-floor)

Source: the legacy 8th Wall sample project `hidermaterial` (A-Frame or three.js), rebuilt in plain three.js on the shared
Labs shell (`../shared/`). What was kept and what changed is written at the top of `main.js`.

Assets (in `../media/`): robot.glb; the pillar texture is drawn on a canvas with the MEDIALIFE wordmark.

Credits: Hider-material technique from the hidermaterial sample (its cube texture came from a third-party CDN and is not used). Robot: RobotExpressive (three.js examples).

Engine: 8th Wall engine binary 1.0.0 (world tracking), three.js r180. Licences are on the Licences page (menu > Licences, `/evade/licences/`), never on screen.

Status: simulator-tested only (headless Chromium, fake camera, faked motion). Not tested on a phone yet.
