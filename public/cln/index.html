02 Behind the seams

The real challenge

Merch that truly reflects a VTuber's community — and holds up to the anime streetwear crowd — is hard to build. Doing it across a whole roster, every season, in a second continent, is a full business of its own.

It means staying true to each talent's world while managing suppliers, artists, quality, cross-border fulfillment and customer service — work that pulls a talent agency away from what it does best.

  • Upfront inventory risk and unsold stock
  • Suppliers, samples and quality control across borders
  • Fulfillment, returns and support for North American fans
  • Merch that's sold once, then disconnected from the channel

04 Activated Merchandise™

Merch that sends fans back to the channel

Our biggest differentiator. An NFC chip and QR code in every product turn a one-time purchase into an ongoing engagement channel. A tap opens an app-free experience in about a second — exclusive content, interactive moments and rewards that pull fans back to the stream and into the next drop.

No app, no download, no account. Content can be updated any time without reprinting a thing.

04

Deliverables & responsibilities

Who does what across the program.

MEDIALIFE™ will deliver

  • Strategy & design: merchandise strategy, creative direction, original artwork and product design for 3–5 SKUs per VTuber
  • Production: sourcing, sampling, manufacturing and quality control
  • Activated Merchandise™: NFC/QR activation in every product, plus an AR experience and call-to-action per VTuber
  • Commerce: a MEDIALIFE-operated storefront, payments, inventory management and fulfillment
  • Support: customer service, returns and post-purchase support
  • Launch & events: launch campaign execution and convention activations as approved
  • The cLaN Hub: talent, campaign, product and asset management, with sales and engagement analytics and monthly reports

cLaN Entertainment will provide

  • IP licensing rights for participating VTubers and approved SKUs
  • The talent and SKU shortlist
  • High-resolution character art, model references, logos and brand guidelines
  • Promotion of each drop through VTuber streams, socials and community channels
  • Collaboration on launch campaigns and promotional activities
  • Creative feedback and approvals within 5 business days

Project timeline

Target milestones from signature. Dates shift with approvals and are confirmed at kickoff.

  1. Phase 01 · Oct – Nov 2026Agreement & onboarding

    SOW signed. cLaN confirms the VTuber and SKU shortlist and licensing approvals. Asset intake opens in the cLaN Hub.

  2. Phase 02 · Nov 2026 – Jan 2027Design & sampling

    Creative direction, artwork and samples per VTuber, plus AR experience concepts. cLaN reviews and approves.

  3. Phase 03 · Feb – Mar 2027Production & store build

    Manufacturing and QC, activation tagging, AR builds and device QA, storefront build and launch campaign planning.

  4. Phase 04 · Apr 2027Evergreen launch

    The cLaN store goes live with the first drop, supported by VTuber launch streams and social pushes.

  5. Phase 05 · May – Aug 2027Convention season

    Anime North, Kawaii Kon (tentative), Anime Expo, Anime Revolution (TBD) and Anime NYC, each with convention-exclusive SKUs and live AR activations as approved.

  6. Phase 06 · OngoingDrops, reporting & growth

    Quarterly drops, monthly reporting and live hub analytics, with a joint performance review six months after launch to plan the next roster expansion.
