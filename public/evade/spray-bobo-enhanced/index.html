How to paint

  1. Spray: your phone is the can. Aim the ring at a piece and hold the screen. Closer is finer, further is wider.
  2. Brush and Erase: paint or rub off with your finger.
  3. Fill: tap a part (an ear, the label, a wheel) to paint all of it.
  4. Stickers: pick one, then tap a piece to stick it on.
  5. Move picks a piece up again, paint and all. Your paint jobs stay on this phone for next time.
