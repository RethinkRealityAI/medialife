Find the can

  1. Point at the Eclipse can on your keychain, about a hand from the camera (either side works).
  2. Tap Play Some Hoops. The can grows. Point your phone at the floor or a table, pinch to size it, then tap Place.
  3. Swipe up to shoot. The camera aims, the length of your swipe is the power. 10 balls: 2 in, 3 for a swish, +1 from over 2.5 m away, the last ball counts double.
