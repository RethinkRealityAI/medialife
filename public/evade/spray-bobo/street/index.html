Take it to the streets

      
    
        
  1. Hang. Point at a wall or the floor. Pinch to size, twist to turn, tap Hang it here. On a wall it's a poster, on the floor it stands up.
    2. 
        
  2. Stamp. Point at anything and tap to stamp your art on it. Walls, floors, tables, boxes.
    3. 
        
  3. Graffiti. No merch needed. Point at a wall and hold the can. Closer sprays thin lines, further back sprays wide. Spray in one spot and it drips.
    4. 
        
  4. Move (bottom right) picks up the last thing you put up, paint and all, so you can put it somewhere else.
    5. 
      

      
Plain walls work best with a bit of floor in view. Move your phone slowly side to side first so it can see the room.
