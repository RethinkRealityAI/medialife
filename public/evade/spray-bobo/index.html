Pick a charm

  1. Find it. Hold Bobo or the Eclipse can in front of the camera, about a hand away, so it fills the middle of the screen. Each one gets its own painting.
  2. Grab the can. When the charm lights up, tap the spray can.
  3. Spray. Aim your phone like a can and hold the can button. Closer sprays thin lines, further back sprays wide.
