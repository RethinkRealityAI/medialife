Pick what to paint

  1. Find it. Hold Bobo or the Eclipse can about a hand from the camera, or step back until the Sukeban print on your tee fills the screen. Each one gets its own painting.
  2. Grab the can. When it lights up, tap the spray can.
  3. Spray. Aim your phone like a can and hold the can button. Closer sprays thin lines, further back sprays wide.
  4. Save and dress up. Your painting comes alive on the charm. Then add decals: shades, hats, collars and stickers.
