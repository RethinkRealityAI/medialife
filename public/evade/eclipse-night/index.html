Three steps to night

  1. Charge the can. Hold your Eclipse can charm about a hand from the camera, either side.
  2. Look up. Point your phone at the open sky. Your real sky turns to night and the can eclipses the moon.
  3. Wake Bobo's stars. Hold the ring over each glowing star until it lights. Join them all and Bobo comes out to play.

Best outside or at a window. Indoors, choose "Everything above" in Options and the ceiling becomes the sky.
