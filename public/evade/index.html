Questions

Quick answers.

What is Activated Merch?

Activated Merchandise® is a physical product that unlocks an immersive experience. Scan the QR or tap the NFC on your EVADE keychain and Cola Run, starring Bobo, opens on your phone. Play it to unlock rewards.

What is Bobo Off the Chain?

The keychain's AR experience. Point your phone camera at the charms and Bobo and the Eclipse can jump off the keychain in 3D. Feed Bobo, play, and film it to share. No app.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Cola Run plays right in your phone's browser, so there's no app to install. Scan or tap and it opens.

Where do I get the keychain?

The Bobo and Eclipse charm set is in the MEDIALIFE shop. Creators can also get one free by joining the EVADE creator crew.

Who is Bobo?

Bobo is EVADE's cat and the star of Cola Run. Fans spot Bobo in AR all over the world, and you can follow along on the Where is Bobo? globe.

I'm a creator. How do I get involved?

Join the EVADE creator crew for a free merch package and giveaway codes for your community.
