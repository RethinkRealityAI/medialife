Beam a photo

  1. Snap a selfie (or switch camera, or pick one from your library), give it a style and press Ready to beam.
  2. Point at a charm. Bobo, the Eclipse can or Roblox Bobo, about a hand from the camera. Its photo spot glows.
  3. Aim and lock on. Move the centre of the screen onto the glowing spot (or tap it). It turns yellow: Beam ready.
  4. Press Beam. It flies in and stays on that charm, on this phone. Beam again to put it on another one.
