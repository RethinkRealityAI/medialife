Beam a photo

  1. Point at a charm. Bobo, the Eclipse can or Roblox Bobo, about a hand from the camera. Either side works.
  2. Tap the glowing spot. Bobo's visor, the can's label or the cap. Or press Photo, left of the shutter.
  3. Snap or choose a picture, give it a style, and press Beam it. Keep the charm in view while it flies in.
  4. It stays on. Each charm keeps its own photo on this phone. Drag across the can to spin the label.
