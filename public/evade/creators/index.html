06 · Questions

Quick answers.

Who can join?

Creators who make Roblox, gaming or lifestyle content on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Twitch, Roblox, Discord or X. If EVADE is your thing, apply.

Do I need a big following?

No. We look at fit and content quality, not only follower count. Small creators with great content are welcome.

What's in the package?

Two activated EVADE keychains, a sticker and a tee, plus free codes to give away to your community.

When will I hear back?

We review every sign-up and reply by email with next steps.
