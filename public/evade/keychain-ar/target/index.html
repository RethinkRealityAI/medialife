Keychain test pictures

For trying the AR without the real keychain. Open the AR page on your phone, then show one of these full screen on a computer and point the phone at it. Turn the screen brightness up and avoid reflections. Each charm is its own keychain on a shared ring, so you can check that each one is found on its own, from different angles and distances.

Flip turns the keychain over: each charm shows its other side (cat Bobo and Roblox Bobo mirrored, as the print looks through the clear acrylic, and the can with its other face), so you can check that the 3D characters turn with it.
