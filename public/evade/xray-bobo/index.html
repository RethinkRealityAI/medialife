Find a charm

  1. Point at a charm. Hold Bobo or the Eclipse can about a hand from the camera (either side works), or step back until Bobo on your tee fills the screen.
  2. Look inside. The print opens into a box. Move your phone around it and look in from the sides: what's inside floats in the middle.
  3. Scan it. Switch between Scan, X-ray and Skin: Bobo shows his bones, the can its soda. Tap to say hi or fizz the can, swipe to turn it.
