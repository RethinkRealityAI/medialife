Welcome to the booth

  1. Face the camera. Hold your phone at arm's length in good light. Friends can squeeze in too.
  2. Swipe to try on. Pick Looks, Hat, Shades, Earrings, Nose or Face paint, then swipe the row: whatever sits in the circle is what you wear.
  3. Face tricks. Open your mouth wide and hold it for cola fizz, raise your eyebrows and hold for the next look, wink and Bobo waves. Keep your head still for a moment.
  4. Shoot. Tap the white button for a photo, hold it to film. Turn on Strip for four shots on a countdown.
