Add it to Gmail (desktop browser)

  1. Press Copy signature above.
  2. In Gmail, open Settings (gear icon) → See all settings → General.
  3. Scroll to Signature. Delete your old one if you have it, then Create new and name it “MEDIALIFE”.
  4. Click in the signature box and paste (Ctrl/⌘ + V).
  5. Under Signature defaults, pick it for new emails and for replies/forwards.
  6. Scroll to the bottom and press Save Changes.

Set it up in Gmail on the web. The Gmail phone apps keep their own plain-text signature, so emails sent from your phone won’t show the animated logo.
