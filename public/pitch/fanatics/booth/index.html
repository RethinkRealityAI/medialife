How the try-on works

  1. Face the camera. Hold your phone at arm's length in good light. A friend can squeeze in too.
  2. Swipe to try on. Whatever sits in the circle is what you wear, or raise both eyebrows for the next hat. Pick Face paint for game-day stripes.
  3. Shoot. Tap the white button for a photo, hold it to film. Turn on Strip for four shots on a countdown.
  4. Like it? Tap Shop to see that hat.

You just tried it on. In a live campaign this opens the product page with your size and the look you shot.
